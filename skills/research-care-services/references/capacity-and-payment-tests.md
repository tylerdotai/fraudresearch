# Capacity and payment comparability

## Before calculating

Record legal entity/site ID; license/exemption and effective dates; age groups; simultaneous capacity versus annual unique children; service days/hours; enrollment/attendance/authorized-slot basis; reimbursable absence or closure rules; rate period and units; parent contributions; grants versus service reimbursement; adjustments/refunds; and the actual paid amount. Each rule requires a source for the relevant jurisdiction and dates.

A maximum capacity is a simultaneous operating constraint, not automatically an annual count of unique children or paid units. A grant may fund capacity, stabilization, quality improvements or another allowed purpose rather than pay for each attendance event. Do not assume the mechanism from a dollar total.

## Comparison gates

| Apparent discrepancy | First check | Evidence needed to advance |
|---|---|---|
| More annual children than licensed slots | Turnover, part-time scheduling, multiple sites, period | Matched service units and applicable capacity rule |
| Payments during closure | Applicable enrollment/absence/closure/grant rules | Specific payment purpose, dates and paid/adjusted status |
| Two providers at one address | Units, landlord/mail service, ownership/site history | Dated site and recipient identity evidence |
| Amount exceeds a published rate | Rate vintage, age group, quality tier, unit, supplements | Correct rate schedule and payment detail |
| Large increase year over year | Program changes, funding rounds, new locations, catch-up payments | Comparable periods and amended award/payment records |

## Synthetic example: unit mismatch

A synthetic annual provider total is compared with a single month's capacity times an assumed daily rate. The result is uninterpretable unless the applicable payment basis, age groups, operating days, time period and funding components are established. Do not publish the excess as a loss. Rewrite the calculation plan with explicit units and actual rules, or stop.

## Deliverable

For each candidate discrepancy retain: exact rule passage; applicable date; observed record and locator; calculation inputs/units; recipient/site relationship; benign explanation; disconfirming source sought; missing decisive evidence. Use `questioned amount` only with an explicit methodology and distinguish it from proven loss. Do not infer intent from a licensing violation or budget variance.
