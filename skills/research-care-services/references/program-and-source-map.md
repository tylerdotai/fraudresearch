# Program and source map

## Authoritative discovery entry points

- Office of Child Care: https://acf.gov/occ
- Child Care and Development Fund: https://acf.gov/occ/child-care-and-development-fund
- Program plans: https://acf.gov/occ/programs-services/child-care-and-development-fund/resources/plans

OCC describes CCDF as a federal funding program administered in collaboration with state, territory and tribal governments. The plans page describes applications/program assurances and links approved plan cycles. This establishes a program-discovery path, not a specific provider's funding or a universal payment rule.

Select the actual approved plan, amendment and effective dates. Do not use an old introductory paragraph or guessed URL as the current payment rule.

## Required layers

| Layer | Find and preserve | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Federal program | Statute/regulations/guidance and award/plan relevant to dates | Program existence is not provider participation |
| Lead administrator | Exact state/territory/tribal lead agency and applicable plan | Do not assume one nationwide rule |
| Provider/site | License or exemption record, site/legal-entity IDs, capacity dates | Licence may not prove subsidy enrollment |
| Funding | Award/subaward/contract/recipient records and pass-through chain | Annual allocation is not a provider payment |
| Payment | Period, unit, service category, amount, corrections, actual paid status | Authorized or claimed amounts can differ from paid |
| Enforcement | Exact entity/conduct/date and procedural stage | Prior allegations are not new findings or proof about neighbors |

## Special services definition gate

Record the official agency/program term, who is served, service unit, payment authority and provider requirements. Do not equate this phrase automatically with special education, disability services, home health or child care. If no authoritative definition is established, retain `definition_required` in the source registry and choose another observable question.

## Access and privacy

The current workbench has no automatic care-services source connector. Generic public ingestion is possible after source qualification; it is not nationwide integration. Public non-.gov sites may require authorized browser inspection or an adapter review. Accounts, fees, formal requests, contacts and restricted records need approval. Do not gather children/family identities merely to fill a data gap.
