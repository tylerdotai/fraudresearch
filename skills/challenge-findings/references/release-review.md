# Substantive release review

## Review layers

1. Source integrity: original and extraction hashes match trusted local metadata.
2. Citation integrity: exact quoted passage exists at the specified locator.
3. Source meaning: passage actually supports the sentence, with headers/units/context.
4. Identity/time: evidence concerns the right entity, relationship and period.
5. Measurement: row grain, denominator, money type, revisions and joins are correct.
6. Interpretation: proposed conduct follows; plausible contrary explanations tested.
7. Originality/program: new contribution and applicable route are honestly characterized.
8. Authority: private review/export only; any external action separately approved.

The CLI checks important parts of layers 1–2 and some structural fields. It cannot decide layers 3–8. A fabricated federal-nexus interpretation can pass if the quote merely exists; that is why analyst review must read the evidence rather than trust a green flag.

## Dispositions

`Supported` means a narrowly stated observation has adequate evidence. `Narrowed` means the original wording exceeded the evidence. `Contradicted` retains the disconfirming record and withdraws the affected claim. `Unresolved` states exactly what remains unknown and how a new record could change it. Do not collapse these into one fraud/confidence number.

## Material blockers

Block stronger reporting for identity collisions, unsupported crosswalks, mismatched periods, unparsed decisive material, missing amount units, unsupported loss figures, source-only allegations presented as findings, absent funding/subject-area basis or misrepresented novelty. A private draft with these gaps is allowed if accurately labelled; missing/tampered source bytes cannot be exported by the current tool.

## Acceptance note

Quote the most consequential supporting and contrary passages. State what checks actually ran and what did not. Do not claim forensic audit, clinical review, legal certification or independent expert review when only an agent/tool workflow was exercised. If review changes material content, invalidate affected old conclusions and rerun relevant checks for the new version.
