# Check Program Fit

## Purpose and boundary

Assess program applicability from current written sources. This is an evidence-based eligibility screen, not legal advice, a reward decision or permission to submit. Broad portal routing and eligibility for the particular early-stage pilot are different questions.

## Reference loading

- Before any CLI call in read-only work, or when command effects are uncertain: [Command effects and strict read-only route](../preserve-evidence/references/command-effects.md).
- Before screening a lead: [Written rules and exclusions](references/rules-and-exclusions.md).
- Before recommending a route or resolving a conflict: [Applicability matrix and rule differences](references/applicability-matrix.md).

## Procedure

1. Read rules-and-exclusions before making any eligibility statement. Refresh official snapshots if currency is material; record source IDs, retrieval dates and the passages relied on. Do not infer a 1% entitlement from an announcement.
2. Fill the applicability matrix: specific conduct/program; relevant funding or health-care basis; individual eligibility; original information; voluntariness; cooperation; timing; and known exclusions. Use supported/unverified/contradicted rather than a combined confidence score.
3. Inspect the complete detailed terms, including subject area, exceptions, anonymity, submission procedure and discretion—not just the introduction. Preserve conflicting versions or clauses in a rule-difference record.
4. Load trace-federal-funds when relying on a federal loss or pass-through program. Do not impose an unsupported blanket federal-dollar requirement on every health-care category; follow the actual wording and flag interpretive uncertainty.
5. Load review-originality for independent public-data analysis. Keep government prior knowledge unknown. Prepare a fit note listing decisive gaps and exact sources; proceed only to private research or review.

## Completion contract

A dated fit note maps each requirement to a quoted source and case fact, with unresolved matters explicit. Portal acceptance, structural export success and analyst belief are not described as award approval. No submission or identity disclosure is performed.
