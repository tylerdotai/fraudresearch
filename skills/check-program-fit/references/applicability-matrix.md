# Applicability and change review

Use this structure for each lead:

| Requirement | Written rule / exact locator | Case evidence / exact locator | Status | Decisive gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conduct and program | Current clause | What conduct is actually observed | supported/unverified/contradicted | Missing act or program identity |
| Funding or applicable health-care basis | Current scope clause | Award, reimbursement or industry relationship | same | Agency mention alone insufficient |
| Original information | Definition and exclusions | Analytical delta and publication review | same | Restated public audit |
| Voluntary / personal eligibility | Current definitions | Known facts, not assumptions | same | Material personal circumstances unknown |
| Cooperation / timing | Current conditions | Ability/status not promised | same | No authority to promise testimony |
| Charges / award discretion | Current trigger | Case stage | normally unverified in research | No charges or award decision established |

Do not total these rows into a fraud probability or guarantee. A screening result may be 'in-scope research theme, award eligibility unknown.' A claim of ineligibility also needs the relevant disqualifying fact, not mere missing evidence.

## Rule-difference record

Record old/new source ID, retrieval date, effective date if stated, exact competing text, affected campaign/case assertions, interpretation alternatives and pending authority. Distinguish a changed web page from a legally effective amendment. Re-evaluate affected claims and keep both originals.

## Synthetic route example

An analyst finds a published audit describing an overpayment. That supports a documented accounting finding. It does not independently establish a crime, new original information or a qualifying program. A separate analysis showing a genuinely new supported scheme may be relevant, but must be evidenced; renaming the audit a 'new tip' does not cure the gap.

## Escalation

If the terms are ambiguous for a consequential filing, flag the exact question for competent advice or authoritative clarification. Do not contact DOJ or submit a test form without approval. Continue independent local research only if it does not depend on resolving that question.
