# Written rules and exclusions

## Source hierarchy

Retrieve and read the current detailed terms and FAQ, then compare the overview and portal:
- https://www.justice.gov/fraud/national-fraud-enforcement-division-criminal-tip-rewards-pilot-program
- https://www.justice.gov/fraud/criminal-tip-rewards-pilot-program-faqs
- https://www.justice.gov/fraud/whistleblower
- https://america.gov/fraud

No rule snapshots or prior research are bundled. Preserve your own dated originals and exact quotations before making a program-fit statement. A social-media commission claim is not a governing instrument. Different reward programs can have different triggers and conditions; do not merge them.

## Assess separately

- Covered conduct and program, applicable health-care basis or documented federal funding link.
- Whether the tipster is an eligible individual under current exclusions.
- Voluntariness, prohibited sources, participation, timing and cooperation requirements.
- Truthful, complete, written original information; include contrary evidence.
- Independent knowledge versus independent public-data analysis and the required analytical contribution.
- Qualifying criminal milestone and agency discretion; no reward is guaranteed.

Ask personal eligibility questions only when necessary for an actual human-directed filing decision. Do not collect unnecessary family, patient or beneficiary information during research. Keep government prior knowledge and reward eligibility unknown.

## Anonymity and conflicting wording

Read the current attorney-representation, identity-verification and confidentiality clauses. Anonymous intake and anonymous reward eligibility are different issues. Never promise absolute confidentiality.

When passages or announcements disagree, preserve both, record versions and exact locators, and identify the unresolved issue. Do not silently select the most favorable interpretation or contact an agency without action-specific approval.
