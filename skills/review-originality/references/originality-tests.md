# Originality tests

Primary rule sources: https://www.justice.gov/fraud/national-fraud-enforcement-division-criminal-tip-rewards-pilot-program and https://www.justice.gov/fraud/criminal-tip-rewards-pilot-program-faqs . Read the current preserved clauses, not this summary alone.

The inspected definitions distinguish independent knowledge from independent analysis; analysis may examine public information but must reveal information not generally known/available. The terms also discuss non-public/previously unknown information, material additions, public allegations and excluded sources. Do not extract the permissive public-analysis sentence while ignoring surrounding conditions or exceptions.

## Classification

| Situation | Research classification | What still needs proving |
|---|---|---|
| Repeats an audit's overpayment statement | Restatement | New conduct/evidence or analytical contribution absent |
| Summarizes multiple news reports of the same case | Restatement | More sources do not necessarily add independent substance |
| Computes a previously unstated total | Unverified analytical delta | Correct units, source grain, materiality and meaning |
| Links dated records through verified IDs to reveal a supported non-obvious relationship | Potential independent analysis | Interpretive validity, disconfirming evidence and all other rule conditions |
| Finds no public coverage | Search result only | Confidential government knowledge remains unknown |

## Common errors

Obscurity is not originality. A new chart is not necessarily new information. Many mirrors are not independent sources. A publicly announced investigation is not proof the analyst's exact additional information is already known, but neither does a different phrasing establish novelty. An original-source exception or material-addition provision must be applied to actual facts, not assumed.

Do not obtain information through unauthorized access, privileged communications or other prohibited routes to improve eligibility. Case preparation must preserve truthfulness, completeness, involvement and contrary evidence rather than excluding facts that reduce perceived reward prospects.

## Review output

For each claim: pre-existing public fact and locator; earliest located publication (not asserted first-ever publication); source lineage; exact new contribution; reproducible method; evidence supporting the delta; contrary explanation; status; remaining eligibility uncertainty. Ultimate reward eligibility and government prior knowledge stay unknown unless authoritatively determined.
