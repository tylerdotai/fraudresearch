# Funding-chain methods

Represent a chain explicitly: federal program/agency → prime recipient → pass-through/subrecipient/contractor → questioned transaction. Some programs reimburse an initially local expenditure; the relevant ultimate loss and rules must be established, not assumed from where the first invoice was submitted.

For every edge record: from/to entity and namespace; relationship type; program/award/transaction ID; source ID/locator/quote; start/end/effective dates; amount type and currency if any; support status; and contradictory evidence. A node existing does not prove an edge between nodes.

## Evidence ladder

1. Agency reference: identifies an agency; no recipient transaction.
2. Published program description: shows authority/purpose; no specific participation.
3. Prime award: connects program and recipient for a period; may not connect a subcontractor or invoice.
4. Subaward/contract: supports the next relationship; matching name alone is insufficient.
5. Payment/reimbursement/invoice/settlement record: supports the relevant money event if identity, dates and status align.
6. Evidence about conduct, falsity, knowledge and resulting loss: separate from the funding chain; none follows automatically from a payment.

## Monetary distinctions

Appropriation/budget authority, obligation, outlay, award ceiling, transaction change, loan face value, subsidy cost, invoice amount, refund and adjudicated loss are different measures. A negative transaction can reduce a prior obligation; a record repeated in award summaries and transactions is not two payments. Preserve gross/net definitions and whether a displayed amount is cumulative or a change.

## Synthetic broken-chain example

A federal award names State Agency S; a local spreadsheet names Provider P. Without a record linking S/program/period to P's questioned transaction, the chain is incomplete. The spreadsheet may support a local payment fact but not its funding source. Report the missing edge rather than pasting the federal award into a documented-nexus field.

## Missing data

A missing subaward record may reflect scope, lag, reporting rules or incomplete search. It does not prove off-book funding or no funding. Log query boundaries, periods, aliases and identifiers used. Do not contact a grant officer, request records or pay for a database without approval.
