# Exact identifiers, roles and time

## Identity gate

Preserve source tokens exactly. Check NPI length, numeric characters, the NPI check digit and placeholders before lookup. A token with an invalid check digit stays invalid even if a name search finds a plausible person. Missing, invalid and fuzzy candidates must not enter the confirmed exact-NPI cohort. NPI assignment itself establishes neither licensure nor billing. CCN, PECOS enrollment ID, PAC ID, state provider ID and license number are different namespaces with many-to-many relationships.

## Exclusion test

Acquire current LEIE and relevant exclusions/reinstatement supplements separately. Current LEIE is not a history of everyone excluded during the study period. A minimum exclusion term ending does not establish reinstatement. Inspect REINDATE, WAIVERDATE and jurisdictional waiver conditions; confirm operative dates using authoritative records. Current-month supplements alone do not establish uninterrupted 2018–2024 coverage.

Join the correct provider role to the exact validated identifier and a supported interval. Use only complete service months fully inside the prohibited interval when the public file is monthly; boundary months remain unresolved without finer dates. A prescribing NPI attributed to a later refill does not prove the prescription was written during exclusion. A billing organization is not necessarily the individual who rendered the service. Confirm actual paid status, adjustments and applicable prohibition before quantifying anything.

## Enrollment/credential test

Require the correct state, program, provider role and historical rules. Check retroactive enrollment, emergency waivers, continued approval during processing, permitted supervised roles, temporary licenses, credential reinstatement, court stays and corrected orders. An order can be public without being final or operative. Current directory omissions do not prove historical ineligibility. Stop if the public identifier projection cannot support the necessary join.

## Falsification ledger

For every candidate record: raw source/row; identifier validation; matched role; exact dates and granularity; applicable prohibition; strongest lawful alternative; source sought to disprove the inference; result; and remaining gap. Use unresolved/contradicted/supported-observation classifications, not a fraud probability. Exclude resolved candidates from escalation while preserving their rejection evidence.
